Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address. That's all — we don't ask for your name, phone number, or any other personal data.

2. How we use your email

We use your email only to send the daily 30Days Game UI challenge, plus the confirmation message when you subscribe. We never sell, rent, or share your email with third parties.

3. Third-party services

To deliver emails we use Brevo (email sending) and to store subscription data we use Supabase (database hosting). These services process your data under their own privacy policies. We only store the minimum needed for the service to work.

4. How long we keep your data

We keep your email while your subscription is active. When you unsubscribe, your data is flagged and no longer used for sending.

5. Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email you receive. If you want your data fully deleted, contact us and we'll remove it. Under the LGPD (Brazil) and GDPR (EU), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use a simple theme preference stored in your browser (localStorage). We don't use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at ui30days@gmail.com.
